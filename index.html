The unusual combination

Clinical training teaches you to understand people. Entrepreneurship teaches you to understand systems. Digital practice sits at the intersection.

I am a psychiatrist by training and an entrepreneur by temperament. My work has moved across healthcare, technology, education, consumer products and community initiatives.

That experience shaped a simple belief: great professionals should not remain invisible simply because they were never taught how to present themselves online.

Today, I am particularly interested in helping psychiatrists and mental-health professionals build a digital presence that is clinically credible, strategically positioned and designed around how patients actually make decisions.
